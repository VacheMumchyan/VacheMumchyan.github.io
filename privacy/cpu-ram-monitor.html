CPU & RAM Monitor — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026 (updated for version 5.2) · App: CPU & RAM Monitor (com.justmakeapp.cpurammonitor) · Developer: JustMakeApps

This policy explains what information the CPU & RAM Monitor Android app ("the app") handles. The app is made by JustMakeApps ("we", "us"). The short version: the system statistics the app shows never leave your phone. The only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising service (AdMob) collects to show ads.

1. Data that stays on your device

To show its screens, notification and widget, the app reads information from your phone's operating system:

  • CPU usage estimates, core clock speeds, CPU governor and thermal status
  • Memory (RAM and swap) usage
  • Battery level, temperature, charging state and current
  • Storage usage
  • Network transfer speeds (amount of data, not its content)
  • Device model, chip name and Android version

This information is processed only on your device. We do not upload it, store it on any server, or share it with anyone. The app has no account, no sign-in and no analytics of its own. Short-term history (for the charts) is kept in the phone's memory, and a few settings (for example, whether you were already asked about notifications) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

If you use the Share button, the app creates a text summary of your current statistics and hands it to the app you choose. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner, an ad after sharing, an optional ad that unlocks 24-hour history, and occasionally an ad when you return to the app. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate an approximate (country/city-level) location
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • Interactions with ads (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped)

The app itself does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.

How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in these regions, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy settings link at the bottom of the Overview screen.
  • Personalized ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (wording varies by device).
  • Notifications: the live statistics notification and the high-CPU / low-memory alerts can be turned off with the notification's Stop button or in Android's notification settings.
  • Deleting data: uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device. We hold no data about you to delete.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state — to load ads and to measure network speed.
  • Notifications — for the live statistics notification and alerts (you are asked first on Android 13 and later).
  • Foreground service — to keep updating statistics in the notification while the app is in the background. You can stop it at any time.
  • Display over other apps (optional) — only for the floating monitor, which you turn on yourself. The bubble shows this phone's statistics on top of other apps; the app does not read, record or interact with anything shown by other apps. You can turn it off in the app, from the Quick Settings tile, or in Android settings.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app itself collects no personal information, there is nothing we hold about any user. If you believe a child's data was collected through ads in the app, contact us and we will help you reach Google.

6. Security

The statistics the app shows are never transmitted by us. Ad requests made by the AdMob SDK use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play release notes.
